OrbitX Club of Zeal College of Engineering & Research is a dynamic student-driven innovation community focused on technology, leadership, and creativity.

About OrbitX

OrbitX Club encourages students to explore emerging technologies, conduct technical workshops, hackathons, coding competitions, and innovation challenges. Our mission is to build future-ready engineers with strong technical and leadership skills.
