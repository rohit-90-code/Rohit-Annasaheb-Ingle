About Zeal College

Welcome to Zeal College of Engineering & Research

Zeal College is one of the leading educational institutions committed to providing quality technical education. Our college focuses on academic excellence, innovation, and overall student development. With experienced faculty members and modern infrastructure, we prepare students to meet global industry standards.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education that fosters innovation, research, and professional excellence while developing responsible citizens.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in engineering and research by nurturing talent and promoting technological advancement.
